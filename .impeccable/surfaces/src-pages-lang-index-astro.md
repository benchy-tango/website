## Scope

Whole site (5 pages × EN/ES). Mode: Persuade (classes and DJ bookings are the conversion); the Visual Art page leans Experience.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A milonga at night: one artist's world lit low, photographs carrying the atmosphere, Playfair setting his name like a programme. Refuses the dance-school template (icon cards, stock dancers, gold flourishes).

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian ground, Warm Ivory type, Muted Gold for the primary action per section plus small accents (the title hairline, labels, active states), per brand-benchy.md "primary buttons, highlights, fine borders, small accents"; never two gold fills competing in one view, Burgundy only as a low stage light behind the closing band, Charcoal for dividers. Playfair Display for names/titles, Montserrat for everything else. Square-ish 3px corners, 1px rules, no cards except service panels.

STORY: Visitor sees Aaron dancing, understands "tango dancer, teacher, DJ, artist", chooses classes or DJ (each CTA opens the form with the enquiry preselected), optionally browses art, then contacts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dancing photo, dark grade from left and bottom; "Aaron Benchimol" at hero scale lower-left, gold hairline drawn under it, subtitle label, two-line intro, gold "Explore Dance & Music" + outline "Discover My Art". Transparent header above.

FORM: Brief-pinned editorial-tango world. Concept roll not run: the owner instructed "no olvides de usar los estilos de brand-benchy.md" (don't forget to use the styles of brand-benchy.md), which pins palette, typefaces, button styles and art direction; the architecture brief pins page structure and motion. No seed key. Signature motion: name rises and the gold compás hairline draws once; photos open with a clip-path curtain on scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
