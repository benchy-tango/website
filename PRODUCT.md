# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), plain CSS, no extra libraries (architecture brief §8–9). Contact form: Netlify Forms (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-11), so the site is deployed on Netlify.

## Users

- **Tango dancers and learners** looking for private lessons, group classes or workshops with Aaron (primary).
- **Organisers of milongas, festivals and private events** looking for a tango DJ (primary).
- **Festival / school / community organisers** looking to book workshops.
- **Art buyers, galleries and curators** interested in Aaron's abstract paintings (secondary).

## Product Purpose

The personal website of Aaron Benchimol. It exists to turn visitors into class students, workshop hosts and DJ bookings, and to give his painting practice visibility. Success = clear enquiries for classes and DJ bookings through the contact form.

## Positioning

One artist across three connected practices: Argentine tango dancer and teacher, tango DJ, and abstract painter. Dance and music lead the business; painting expands the artistic world around him.

## Capabilities and Constraints

## Product Principles

1. Classes and DJ bookings are always the clearest action on the page.
2. One artist, one identity: dance, music and painting feel like one world, never three businesses.
3. Only real content: missing facts stay missing (or clearly marked as placeholders), never invented.
4. Every enquiry path leads to the contact form with the right enquiry type already chosen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visible keyboard focus, readable contrast on the dark palette, descriptive alt text, reduced-motion support, usable without JavaScript (architecture brief §7, §12).
